### Building 'Garbage': A Music Node

Meet 'Garbage', a music streaming node crafted from leftover parts and a Raspberry Pi 3 A+. This project uses Snapcast for synchronized streaming, incorporating old PC speakers, a DSP hat, and a 15W audio amp. It's a testament to resourcefulness and creativity in tech projects.

**Source →** [drgerg.com](https://www.drgerg.com/category/projects.html)
