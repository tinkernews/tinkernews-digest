*   **Standout Details:**
    *   **Controller:** Raspberry Pi 3 A+.
    *   **Components:** Old PC speakers, a DSP hat, a 15W audio amplifier.

*   **Why Makers Care:** This is a prime example of resourceful making, or "upcycling." It shows how to build a practical, multi-room audio system on a budget by creatively combining salvaged parts with a Raspberry Pi and open-source software.
